Current Work

At Columbia University, I focus on research and innovation as a Research Assistant for two labs. In the Computer Enabled Abilities Laboratory, I develop immersive AR solutions to enhance accessibility and preserve community history. In the Computer Graphics and User Interfaces Lab, I work on geospatial data visualization and XR applications, bridging 2D and 3D environments with technologies like Unity, Blender, and React.

Beyond research, I’ve contributed to NLP and ML projects, including gesture recognition and credit risk assessment, combining technical expertise with creativity to solve real-world challenges. During Summer 2024, I gained industry experience as a Software Development Engineer Intern at a fintech startup, where I built dynamic React components, automated workflows with an OCR-based system, and optimized performance using state management tools.

Background

I began my educational journey at Zion Matriculation Higher Secondary School in Chennai and continued at Narayana Junior College in Hyderabad, where I developed a passion for computer science. I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Gokaraju Rangaraju Institute of Engineering and Technology, graduating as the college topper. My coursework included Algorithms, Programming (C, C++, Java, Python), Databases, Operating Systems, and Compiler Design, providing a strong technical foundation.

Currently, I am pursuing a Master’s in Computer Science at Columbia University, where my studies focus on Computer Vision, NLP, AR/VR, Algorithms, Data Visualization, and UI Design. Alongside my coursework, I serve as a Teaching Assistant for Python for Data Analysis, UI Design, and Databases, which has honed my technical and mentoring skills.
